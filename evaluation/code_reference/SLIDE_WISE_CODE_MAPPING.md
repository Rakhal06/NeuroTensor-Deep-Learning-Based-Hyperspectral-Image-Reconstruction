## Slide 2 — From Deep Image Prior to DeepTensor
- **Slide Title:** FROM DEEP IMAGE PRIOR TO DEEPTENSOR
- **Main Concept:** 
  - Deep Image Prior (DIP): $\hat{X} = f_\theta(Z), \quad \theta^* = \arg\min_\theta \|f_\theta(Z) - X\|_2^2$. A randomly initialized untrained neural network serves as an implicit regularizer.
  - DeepTensor: Extends DIP to low-rank tensor decomposition $\hat{X} = f_U(Z_U) f_V(Z_V)^T$ (matrix/spatial-spectral) and $\hat{\mathcal{X}} = \sum_{r=1}^R u_r \circ v_r \circ w_r$ (CP tensor). Each factor is generated by a separate deep prior network.
- **Related Files:**
  1. `src/deeptensor/models/deep_prior.py` (DIP network factory and downsamplers)
  2. `src/deeptensor/models/backbones/skip.py` (U-Net skip architecture implementation)
  3. `src/deeptensor/models/deeptensor.py` (DeepTensor model coupling spatial and spectral factor networks)
  4. `src/deeptensor/utils/utils_legacy.py` (Random noise input generation `get_inp`, positional encodings)
- **Classes / Functions:**
  - `get_net(...)` in `src/deeptensor/models/deep_prior.py` (Lines 75–150)

  - `get_inp(...)` in `src/deeptensor/utils/utils_legacy.py` (Lines 330–339)
- **File Roles & Interactions:**
  `utils_legacy.py` generates fixed uniform/Gaussian latent noise tensors $Z_U, Z_V$. `deep_prior.py` calls `skip.py` to instantiate the generator networks $f_U, f_V$. `deeptensor.py` feeds $Z_U, Z_V$ through $f_U, f_V$ to generate factor matrices $U, V$, and multiplies them via `torch.bmm(U, V)` to reconstruct the target.

## Slide 3 — Why DeepTensor Over Traditional Methods?
- **Slide Title:** WHY DEEPTENSOR OVER TRADITIONAL METHODS?
- **Main Concept:** Limitations of classical linear low-rank methods (SVD, PCA) vs. nonlinear deep network priors. SVD/PCA assume linear low-rank subspaces and Gaussian i.i.d. noise; they degrade significantly under non-Gaussian noise (Poisson, Rician, Salt & Pepper). DeepTensor uses implicit architectural regularization to reconstruct nonlinear structures without requiring explicit hand-crafted regularizers.
- **Related Files:**
  1. `src/deeptensor/baselines/spectral.py` (Classical Truncated SVD baseline)
  2. `experiments/reproduction/figure06_synthetic_robustness/run_figure6.py` (Noise robustness benchmark across Gaussian, Poisson, and Rician distributions)

## Slide 5 — How DeepTensor Learns the Factors of a Tensor
- **Slide Title:** HOW DEEPTENSOR LEARNS THE FACTORS OF A TENSOR
- **Main Concept:** Data flow of unsupervised factor learning:
  1. Input Tensor $\mathcal{X}$ (corrupted, undersampled, or noisy observation).
  2. Deep Prior Networks (initialized randomly, taking fixed random noise $Z$).
  3. Factor Generation ($f_U(Z_U) \to U, f_V(Z_V) \to V, f_W(Z_W) \to W$).
  4. Self-Supervised Optimization (combining factors into $\hat{\mathcal{X}}$ and minimizing measurement loss $\mathcal{L}(\mathcal{A}(\hat{\mathcal{X}}), \mathcal{Y})$ via Adam).
- **Related Files:**
  1. `src/deeptensor/models/deeptensor.py` (Contraction and factor generation module)
  2. `src/deeptensor/losses/objectives.py` (Measurement loss functions: L1, L2/Frobenius)

- **Classes / Functions:**

  - Training loop in `experiments/reproduction/figure09_hyperspectral/run_figure9.py#L170-L240`
- **File Roles & Interactions:**
  The experiment runner loads data, feeds noise into the generator networks in `deeptensor.py`, calculates loss via `objectives.py`, and triggers PyTorch backpropagation (`loss.backward()`) to update the weights of $f_U, f_V, f_W$.

## Slide 7 — CP/PARAFAC Decomposition
- **Slide Title:** TENSOR DECOMPOSITION TECHNIQUE USED IN DEEPTENSOR
- **Main Concept:** Mathematical formulation of CANDECOMP/PARAFAC (CP) tensor decomposition:
  $$\hat{\mathcal{X}} \approx \sum_{r=1}^R \lambda_r \, (u_r \circ v_r \circ w_r), \quad u_r \in \mathbb{R}^I, \; v_r \in \mathbb{R}^J, \; w_r \in \mathbb{R}^K$$
  Outer product $\circ$, rank $R$, rank-1 component summation, and factor matrices $\mathbf{U} \in \mathbb{R}^{I \times R}, \mathbf{V} \in \mathbb{R}^{J \times R}, \mathbf{W} \in \mathbb{R}^{K \times R}$.
- **Related Files:**
  1. `src/deeptensor/models/deeptensor.py` (`DeepTensorCP` module)
  2. `src/deeptensor/utils/tensor_ops.py` (Tensor contraction and matricization operations)
- **Classes / Functions:**

- **Exact CP Reconstruction Line:**

  ```python
  recon = torch.einsum('r, ir, jr, kr -> ijk', scale, u_fac, v_fac, w_fac)
  ```

## Slide 23 — Adaptive Rank Selection: Motivation & Approach
- **Slide Title:** ADAPTIVE RANK SELECTION (Motivation & Approach)
- **Main Concept:** Dynamic rank discovery during training:
  - Motivation: Fixed rank $R$ requires manual trial and error. Underestimating rank causes underfitting; overestimating rank causes slow convergence and parameter bloat.
  - Proposed Approach: Start with a small rank ($R_\text{start}=4$). Monitor reconstruction loss using Exponential Moving Average (EMA). When learning plateaus ($\Delta \mathcal{L} < \epsilon$), dynamically expand rank by $R_\text{step}=2$ up to $R_\text{max}=30$.
- **Related Files:**
  1. `experiments/extensions/adaptive_rank/run_figure9_adaptive.py` (Adaptive Rank engine)
  2. `src/deeptensor/training/adaptive_rank.py` (Plateau detection and rank growth module)

## Slide 25 — Group Sparsity: Factor Regularization
- **Slide Title:** GROUP SPARSITY: FACTOR REGULARIZATION
- **Main Concept:** Mathematical formulation of structured Factor Group-Sparsity (Group Lasso) for continuous top-down rank pruning:
  $$\mathcal{L}_\text{total} = \mathcal{L}_\text{reconstruction} + \lambda \, \mathcal{L}_\text{group}, \quad \mathcal{L}_\text{group} = \sum_{r=1}^{R_\text{max}} \sqrt{\|U_{:,r}\|_2^2 + \|V_{r,:}\|_2^2}$$
  - Motivation: Over-parameterize with $R_\text{max}=30$ and penalize redundant factor pairs downward.
  - Lambda Grid Search: $\lambda \in \{0, 0.0001, 0.001, 0.01\}$.
- **Related Files:**
  1. `experiments/extensions/group_sparsity/run_figure9_sparsity.py` (Implementation script)
  2. `src/deeptensor/losses/group_sparsity.py` (Group regularizer module)
  3. `temp_pdf_images/slide_25_img_0.png` (Slide schematic)
- **Classes / Functions:**
  - `compute_group_regularizer(U, V)` in `experiments/extensions/group_sparsity/run_figure9_sparsity.py#L83-L120`
- **File Roles & Interactions:**
  `compute_group_regularizer` computes the joint Euclidean norm across the $r$-th spatial column $U_{:,r}$ and spectral row $V_{r,:}$, then sums norms across all $r \in [1, R_\text{max}]$. This penalty promotes joint column sparsity, forcing unnecessary factors to collapse to zero.
- **Implementation Status:** Complete implementation.
- **Important Code Locations:**
